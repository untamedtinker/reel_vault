Getting Started

How Reel Vault Works

01

Log In & Sync

Make sure you are logged into Instagram, then click Sync from either the extension or dashboard. You can keep browsing freely while Reel Vault pulls your saved reels, collections, and captions in the background.

02

Connect Local Storage

Browsers clear cache, but your files shouldn't. Click Storage in the dashboard to link a folder on your drive. Reel Vault will auto-save your library locally as vault.json, keeping your collection safe, offline, and permanently yours.

03

Filter & Creator Feeds

Explore your saved reels across dedicated tabs. Use the Posts tab to filter by Folder, Category, or Tag, and open any Creator to pull up only the reels you've saved from them.
